05 Competition

~22 s. "Others cover the listing and the lease. OpenRent in the UK and Rendin in Estonia list the home, and they, like Zent and Owntly here in Lithuania, handle screening, contracts and rent. Landlords pay for it." ▸ click "But the hours go in between: answering applicants and arranging viewings. None of them books viewings for the landlord." ▸ click "That is where Nuomity starts, from the first application to the signed contract, and rental management comes next." If asked: OpenRent publishes the listing and handles referencing (add-on), digital tenancy agreements and rent collection; enquiries go to the landlord, who arranges and conducts viewings. Rendin: free listing with ID-screened candidates, rent guarantee instead of a deposit, digital contracts. Zent (an Aruodas.lt project): insurance instead of a deposit, credit check, e-contract, rent paid out on schedule. Owntly: starts once a tenant is found; screening, e-signing, rent tracking, utilities, chat. Aruodas.lt is a listing portal, not a competitor: enquiries arrive as messages, so it is a channel for us. Abroad, EliseAI (US) automates leasing with AI for large operators. Sources: OpenRent (Wikipedia, 2022; August 2026 review), Rendin (Vestbee, 2024; rendin.ee), zent.lt, owntly.com (Oct 2026).
